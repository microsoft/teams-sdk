# M365 Extensions

M365 Extensions let you extend an existing Teams SDK application to channels such as Web Chat and Email without rewriting any Teams-specific capabilities.

Add a Microsoft Agents SDK `AgentApplication` as the multichannel host, then register your existing Teams SDK handlers on the bridged `App` returned by `useTeamsSdk` (TypeScript) or `use_teams_sdk` (Python).

Matching Teams activities continue through your Teams SDK handlers, while non-Teams activities and unmatched Teams activities flow through the Agents SDK application.

## How to Use It

<LanguageInclude section="setup" />

M365 Extensions work with your existing Teams SDK configuration and handlers. Register your Teams-specific routes on the returned Teams SDK app and your multichannel routes on the Agents SDK app.

## Key Benefits

### Extend Your Teams SDK App to Multiple Channels

Connect your existing Teams SDK application to channels such as Web Chat and Email without rewriting your Teams-specific logic.

| Activity | Route |
| --- | --- |
| Teams activity matching a Teams SDK handler | Teams SDK |
| Teams activity without a matching Teams SDK handler | Agents SDK |
| Web Chat / Direct Line activity | Agents SDK |
| Email activity | Agents SDK |

## How It Works

M365 Extensions adds a lightweight middleware bridge to your existing Teams SDK application. Aside from app initialization, your Teams-specific code stays the same.

1. **Add the bridge.** Call `useTeamsSdk` (Typescript) or `use_teams_sdk` (Python) to connect your app to Agents SDK.
2. **Keep your Teams logic.** Existing handlers and API client calls continue handling Teams activities.
3. **Add more channels.** Web Chat, Email, and unmatched Teams activities flow to the Agents SDK.
